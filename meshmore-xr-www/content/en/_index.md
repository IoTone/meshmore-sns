Meshmore XR turns a MeshCore LoRa radio and a pair of optical see-through
glasses into a spatial view of your mesh. No phone in your hand, no map on a
screen — the nodes stand in the room around you, at their true bearings, at a
glance.

## What it does

- **The Horizon.** Every node your radio can hear, placed around you by real
  compass bearing and range. Live nodes hold the ring; a quiet mesh looks
  quiet.
- **Nine themes, three postures.** A room-scale field, an active mode that
  moves with you on a hike or a ride, and a tabletop diorama that anchors to a
  real desk and remembers it.
- **Find anyone.** Search by name or key, star your friends' nodes, tag the
  ones from that trip — the ways people actually remember radios.
- **Messages, hands-free.** Threads read aloud per channel if you ask, replies
  by dictation or one-pinch canned responses, a wrist reel for triage.
- **Channels by voice.** Create a channel with a rolled two-word name and share
  it by saying it out loud — no keyboard required, honest about what a spoken
  key can and cannot keep private.
- **Speaks Japanese.** 日本語 throughout, at kanji-legible sizes — not a
  translation pass, a launch language.

## What it needs

- Android XR glasses (built and tested on the XREAL Aura)
- A [MeshCore](https://meshcore.co.uk) LoRa companion radio over Bluetooth
- Nothing else. **No account, no server, no internet permission** — see the
  [privacy policy](/privacy/), which is short because there is nothing to
  disclose.

## Where it stands

In active development against a live 350-node mesh, with a Japan launch in
preparation. Follow this page for the release.
