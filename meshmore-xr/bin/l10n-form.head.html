Two things this app does that a normal Android app does not. The text is drawn on see-through glasses, often outdoors, by someone whose hands are busy — so terse is correct and polite ます／です endings usually cost room better spent on the noun. And every surface is sized once, so text that overruns is cut, not wrapped. Each row says how much room it has, measured in cells: a kanji is 2, a Latin letter 1.
